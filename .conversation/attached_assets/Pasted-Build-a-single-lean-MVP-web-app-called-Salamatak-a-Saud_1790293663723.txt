Build a single lean MVP web app called "Salamatak" — a Saudi platform for
reverse recruitment of Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) professionals.

IMPORTANT — BUILD EFFICIENTLY, THIS IS A SCOPED MVP:
Build this as ONE static frontend app (plain HTML/CSS/JS or a single React
app — your choice, whichever you can build fastest and most reliably) using
mock/local data only — no real backend, no database, no real authentication,
no payment processing. All "accounts," "offers," and "subscriptions" should
be simulated with in-memory or localStorage state so the flows are fully
demonstrable without any server-side work. Do not over-engineer: build
exactly the scope below in one pass, favor simple reusable components over
custom one-off designs, and do not add features beyond what's listed here.

CONCEPT: Companies search an anonymized database of OHS candidates and send
direct job offers. Candidates never search or apply for jobs — they just
register once and complete their profile. Offers are "double-blind" in BOTH
directions: the candidate never sees the company's identity, and the company
never sees the candidate's identity, until the candidate accepts an offer —
at which point a mutual match reveals both identities to each other.

THIS IS NOT A JOB BOARD. Do not build: public job listings, "Apply" buttons,
candidate application queues, public candidate names, or public company names
on offers anywhere in the app.

CORE MVP SCOPE (build all of this):

1. Landing Page
   - Hero with the one-line pitch and two CTAs: "I'm a Company" / "I'm an HSE
     Specialist"
   - "How It Works" 4-step visual: Create Profile → Company Searches → Blind
     Offer → Mutual Match
   - Short problem/solution section (OHS hiring today relies on LinkedIn/
     WhatsApp/generic job boards instead of a specialized platform)
   - Tagline shown prominently: "You don't search for jobs. You create your
     professional profile. Companies search for you. Companies make the
     offer."

2. Candidate Signup & Profile
   - Form: full name, email, phone, city, years of experience, certifications
     (multi-select: NEBOSH, IOSH, OSHA, + "Other" text field), sector, desired
     salary range, and an availability status (Actively looking / Open to
     opportunities / Not actively looking)
   - Profile completion progress bar (e.g. "Profile 85% Complete") — profile
     only counts as "searchable" once 100% complete
   - Candidate dashboard: profile completeness, and a list of received offers
     as "Confidential Opportunity" cards showing position, location, salary,
     benefits, start date — NO company name/logo/contact until accepted.
     Accept/Decline buttons on each pending offer.
   - On Accept: reveal the company's name and contact info in that offer card
     ("It's a Match!"). On Decline: archive the offer, no data shared.

3. Company Signup & Dashboard
   - Registration form: company name, business email, phone, sector, city
   - EMAIL VALIDATION: reject email domains gmail.com, yahoo.com,
     hotmail.com, outlook.com, live.com, icloud.com, aol.com (free/personal
     providers) with a clear error asking for a business domain email instead
   - MANDATORY SUBSCRIPTION GATE: after the form, the company must select one
     of 3 packages (Basic / Pro / Enterprise, placeholder pricing/features)
     before the dashboard unlocks. No payment processing needed — just make
     package selection a required step to "activate" the account.
   - Search/filter panel: certification, years of experience, city, sector
   - Results shown as anonymized cards, e.g. "OHS Professional #10482 — HSE
     Engineer · 7 yrs experience · Riyadh · NEBOSH IGC ✓ · OSHA ✓ ·
     Construction · Available within 30 days" — never a real name, photo, or
     contact info.
   - "Send Offer" button → form: salary, benefits, city, start date, short
     message. The company's own identity stays hidden from the candidate
     until a match occurs.
   - On candidate Accept: reveal the candidate's real name/contact info to
     the company ("It's a Match!").

4. About / FAQ Page
   - Explain the reverse recruitment model and the two-way (mutual) privacy
     guarantee in plain language

LANGUAGE: Primary/default language is English. Add a simple EN/AR toggle in
the navbar that switches visible text to Arabic with RTL layout. Keep the
Arabic translation lightweight (translate the main UI strings; it doesn't
need to be exhaustive for the MVP).

DESIGN: Clean, professional, B2B/trustworthy tone — not a casual job board.
Navy blue + safety yellow/orange accent palette. Fully responsive. Simple
shared navbar/footer across all pages.

DO NOT BUILD YET (out of scope for this MVP — just keep the data model
loosely flexible enough to add these later, but don't spend time building
them now):
   - Admin role/dashboard
   - Certification verification workflow (Verified/Pending/Not Verified)
   - Real backend, database, authentication, or payment integration
